# Magazine Pitch Prompt (Example)

Help me create a short pitch for a magazine feature.  
The story explores how small creative businesses are using AI tools to streamline workflows, improve customer engagement, and launch digital products more efficiently.

Focus on:

- a compelling hook  
- clear angle and audience takeaway  
- why the story matters now  
- a professional pitch structure suitable for editors  

Use the Dream Publisher GPT output format:

1. Summary (≤4 lines)  
2. Top 3 Action Steps  
3. Optional Decisive Question
